Fishing Tycoon

A 3D browser tycoon game built with three.js. Pilot a boat across open water, cast into timed fishing zones, sell your catch at the dock, and clear contract quotas to progress. Includes a full upgrade tree — inventory capacity, cast speed, boat engine, and research — plus a win state once every contract is cleared.
